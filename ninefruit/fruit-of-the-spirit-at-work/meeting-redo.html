The meeting redo

Seven minutes for a pair or small group. Read Galatians 5:22–23 in your preferred Bible. Use patience and gentleness to practise one difficult sentence.

1 minute · Hear the moment

In a handover meeting, Rowan starts to explain a delay. Lee interrupts: “We already know why. Can we move on?” Rowan closes the notebook. The team still needs a revised delivery time.

Ask: Which action did you actually see or hear? Which motive would you only be guessing?

2 minutes · Compare two restarts

Lee notices the interruption

“Rowan, I cut you off. What was the part we needed to hear before we set the new date?”

What does this give Rowan room to do?

A teammate speaks up

“I need the new date too. Rowan, could you finish your point? Then we can decide what changes.”

Which words keep the deadline in view?

3 minutes · Rehearse and switch

One person plays Rowan. The other chooses either speaker and tries a restart in their own words. Rowan answers: “The delivery moved to Thursday. I can send a revised schedule by noon.” Switch roles and try a shorter restart.

Observer prompt: Name the exact words that gave Rowan a turn. If a sentence guessed at someone's intentions, try it again using only what happened.

My restart:

1 minute · Take one sentence with you

Which part was hardest: owning the interruption, inviting the other person back in, or returning to the decision? Keep one sentence you could genuinely say. Participants may observe or pass.
