Read the whole scene.

Six fictional situations. Choose a reading, then compare its explanation. No timer, grade, account or saved answers.

Group prompt: which words support your choice? Let someone explain before selecting an answer. These scenes are original examples, not Scripture or a test of faith.

Carry these checks into a reading
  1. Read the surrounding passage.
  2. Identify the speaker and audience.
  3. Keep conditions and connecting words.
  4. Notice the form of writing.
  5. Separate an observation from an inference.

For a group, ask each reader for one detail they noticed and one question still open. Do not require personal disclosure.
