Where do you stall?

Five questions. Nothing is stored or sent.

1. Last time you tried, what happened?

2. How much time do you have, truthfully?

3. When you read a chapter, what do you want at the end?

4. How do you feel about explanations and commentary?

5. Which sounds most like the book you would finish?

    Honest limits

    One translation.

    The World English Bible, public domain, quoted word for word. No ESV, NIV, NASB or NRSV inside the app. If your church reads one of those, you read alongside.

    Six study books, 21 chapters.

    Ruth, Jonah, Philippians, James, Habakkuk, Philemon. The full Bible is there to read, but the question-and-trace study exists for those six.

    Four of the six are paid.

    One-time Premium Lifetime, no subscription, per the listing. Ruth and Jonah stay free, and that is where this page sends you anyway.

    Map tiles need a connection.

    The reader, studies and notes are offline; the Apple Maps tiles are not. New app (October 2026), iPhone on iOS 18 or newer.

    Who should skip it

    • You need your own translation inside the app. See the first limit.
    • You want a devotional. A daily thought with a verse is a different thing from study, and Wayfold does not do it.
    • You want a whole-Bible curriculum. Six books is a start, not a syllabus.
    • You are on Android or iOS 17. iPhone, iOS 18 or newer.

    Questions people ask next

    Where should I start reading the Bible on my own?

    With a short book you can finish in a week, not with Genesis 1 and a promise to read straight through. Ruth is four chapters and one family's story; Jonah is four chapters and one reluctant prophet. In Wayfold both are free, with their questions and leader guides, per the listing.

    How do I know if I understood a chapter?

    Try to say what happened, in order, and point to the verse for each event. If you cannot, read it again. That is the method Wayfold builds in: answer a few questions, then see the exact words in the chapter that answer each one, then put the main events in order, each tied to its verse.

    Which translation does Wayfold use?

    The World English Bible, which is public domain, quoted word for word. If your group reads ESV, NIV or another translation, you will be reading alongside it rather than in it. That is a real limit; see below.

    Is it Protestant or Catholic?

    Both canons are included, 66 and 73 books, and you choose yours, per the listing. Explanations are labelled In the text, History, Interpretation, Traditions differ or Debated, so you can see where an explanation is a reading rather than the text.

    What is free and what is paid?

    Free: the Ruth and Jonah studies and leader guides, the complete Bible reader with read-aloud and search, the map with all 64 places, the timeline, 4 of 10 journeys, 7 of 63 background lessons. Premium Lifetime is one optional one-time purchase, no subscription: Philippians, James, Habakkuk and Philemon with their guides, all journeys and lessons, highlights and notes.

    Can I use it to lead a group?

    The listing describes printable leader guides (PDF) for a chapter or a whole book: introduction, outline in order, questions with an answer key and verses, a background note, places named, a reflection question. Print, save to Files, AirDrop or email. Ruth and Jonah guides are free.
