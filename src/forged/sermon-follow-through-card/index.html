MAKE THE RESULT USEFUL

A review is more useful than a forgotten promise.

  1. Capture: write your takeaway in your own words. Record the passage separately so your summary is not mistaken for a quotation.
  2. Choose: name an action you can recognise when it happens. “Be kinder” is broad; “listen without interrupting during tonight’s conversation” is observable.
  3. Return: hide the card, recall the main point, then reveal it. Note what you tried and what you would change next time.

Keep any actual scripture quotation accurate and in context. This tool does not evaluate theology, send reminders or store your notes. After recall, you can copy or download the original card together with any reflection you want to keep.
