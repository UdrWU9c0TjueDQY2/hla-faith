How to practice a Bible verse with first letters

  1. Choose a short passage. Copy it carefully from your Bible and include the reference and translation in the label.
  2. Read the full wording. Look at the nearby passage too; recall should not separate a line from its context.
  3. Use the prompts you need. Try alternating gaps, then initials. A harder mode is optional.
  4. Check against the full text. Use the mode menu to bring the exact entered wording back. Correct a mistake before repeating it.
  5. Return another day. This page does not schedule reviews or measure long-term retention.

What the tool does with punctuation

Spaces, line breaks and punctuation remain. Each letter-or-number word becomes either visible text, a first-letter prompt or a blank. Apostrophes inside words stay together. It does not fetch a translation, verify quotations, listen to your voice or judge your memory.

Keep the practice small enough to repeat

A single passage you revisit is a practical starting point. You can rehearse silently or aloud. No timer, streak warning or score is needed here. The sample is an original practice sentence, not a Bible quotation.
