Choose something appropriate to the relationship and setting. Ask before taking over a task, sharing information or changing someone else's space.

MY ONE SMALL PRACTICE

Kindness in ordinary language.

Notice.

Look for a real need or contribution. A specific thank-you says more than a performance of being nice.

Offer.

Make the action clear and the limit honest. Let the other person decide whether it helps.

Follow through.

Do the small thing you chose. If circumstances change, explain that plainly rather than leaving someone guessing.
