Scene six · Thursday, made up but familiar

One Thanksgiving, six moments

9:10 amThe oven is on and the first text arrives: “Running late, bringing Dave.” Nobody invited Dave. You write the three things you cannot control on the back of the shopping list and hand the rolls to your sister.

1:40 pmDave has opinions. Your one sentence is ready before the gravy: “I’d rather hear about your year, Dave.” It turns out his year was hard. The dam holds.

2:15 pm“So when are you two having kids?” You say the line you practised in the car and ask about the knee. The knee takes eleven minutes.

3:30 pmYour brother mentions the house. You count ten seconds. You say back what you heard. You decide it is not today, and that is a decision, not a dodge.

4:05 pmSomeone says Mum’s name while cutting the pie. The room goes quiet for a moment and then the stories come. The chair is still empty; it is less heavy.

1:02 amThe cart has a stand mixer in it. You close the tab. In the morning you still want it; you buy it at noon and it is on sale anyway.

Scene seven · questions
Which Bible verses help with difficult family members?

The ones that speak to a specific moment rather than to “family” in general: Proverbs 17:14 for stopping a quarrel before it starts, Colossians 4:6 for the intrusive question, James 1:19 for the old wound, Philippians 4:6 for the exhausted host, Psalm 34:18 for grief at the table, Romans 12:18 for the drive home. Pick the situation above to see each with a plain why and one step.

What translation are these?

The World English Bible, public domain, quoted word for word. Lampstep uses the same translation for every verse, per its listing. If your church reads ESV, NIV or another translation, the wording will differ slightly.

What does Lampstep do that this page does not?

Per the listing: a daily Fork (a real-life moment with three or four honest choices, then the verse, a plain why and the best move), an SOS verse in 90 seconds that is always free, an evening check-in, and Tomorrow Prep, which gives you the verse and a step 15 minutes before a moment you name. One Tomorrow Prep is free; unlimited with Plus.

Is Lampstep free?

Free forever, per the listing: SOS, today’s Fork, the evening check-in and Proverb of the Day. Lampstep Plus is an optional purchase for every Fork, all eight Paths, unlimited Ready Verses and Tomorrow Prep: $49.99 a year with a 14-day free trial, $69.99 a year for a family of up to six, $9.99 a month, or $129.99 once. Prices in USD.

Is it private?

The listing says no account, no AI, no servers; answers and reflections never leave the phone.

What if the hard moment is bigger than a dinner?

Then the dinner is not the problem to solve. Lampstep says it is not a substitute for professional help. In the US, call or text 988 for crisis support; the app shows 988 and emergency resources first in its crisis moments.
