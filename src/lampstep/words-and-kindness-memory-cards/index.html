Keep practice small

  1. Read one verse and its surrounding passage.
  2. Try the two cards for that verse. Use a hint or reveal whenever useful.
  3. Say the whole verse with its reference once, then stop or continue at your own pace.
  4. Come back another day without treating a forgotten word as a failure.

What the recap means

“Entered correctly” means you supplied the matching word without revealing it on that card. It does not prove memorization: you may have used a hint or read the source. “Completed after reveal” means the answer was shown before that card was completed. Neither is a measure of faith, understanding or spiritual progress.

Keep the wording and the meaning together

A missing-word exercise is a starting point. Read the paragraph around each verse and consider a concrete response in its context. Do not use an isolated phrase to settle a complicated situation.
